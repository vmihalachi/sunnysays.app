Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

sunnysays is a desk companion for Mac and Windows by Vlad Mihalachi. This policy covers the app and this website. The short version: the app collects nothing.

The app

  • It never accesses your camera or microphone. To stay quiet during calls, it only checks whether another app is using them.
  • It makes no network requests of its own. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • It stores only your settings, and whether you’re a Supporter, on your computer. It doesn’t keep what Sunny said, when, or what you did.
  • If you turn on the notification channel, macOS asks once for permission to show notifications, and Windows follows your notification settings. They are shown on your computer and go nowhere else.
  • The Supporter purchase and tips are processed by Apple through the App Store on the Mac, and by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store on Windows. The Windows version is also installed and updated through the Microsoft Store. We receive no payment details and nothing that identifies you. The app asks the store whether you own Supporter and remembers the answer with your settings, so it can thank you instead of asking. It keeps no record of tips.

This website

This site sets no cookies and loads no trackers, analytics or third-party scripts. It remembers your light or dark choice, and the voice you picked for Sunny, in your browser’s local storage, which never leaves your device. Our host, Cloudflare, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for a short time to run and protect the service. We don’t use them to identify anyone.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
